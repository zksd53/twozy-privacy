<section>
  <h2>2. Information We Collect</h2>
  <p>
    When you create an account, HeartLuma processes
    your email address, authentication information
    and account identifiers.
  </p>
  <p>
    We also process information necessary to operate
    your private partner connection, including couple
    membership, invitation records and connection
    timestamps.
  </p>
  <p>
    When you send a note, HeartLuma stores its text,
    sender and recipient identifiers, associated couple,
    timestamps and information needed to process
    requests reliably.
  </p>
  <p>
    Service providers may process technical information,
    including IP addresses, connection information
    and security or operational records.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>3. How We Use Information</h2>
  <p>
    HeartLuma uses information to create and authenticate
    accounts, maintain private partner connections,
    deliver notes, operate widgets, prevent unauthorized
    access, troubleshoot problems and protect the service.
  </p>
  <p>
    We do not use private notes for advertising.
    The reviewed app does not include a configured
    third-party advertising or product-analytics SDK.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>4. Private Notes</h2>
  <p>
    HeartLuma lets connected partners exchange short,
    private notes of up to 160 characters.
  </p>
  <p>
    Notes are stored using Supabase infrastructure
    and protected by authenticated access controls
    and database permissions.
  </p>
  <p>
    Notes are not end-to-end encrypted.
    Authorized service operators and infrastructure
    providers may have privileged access when necessary
    to operate, maintain or protect the service.
  </p>
  <p>
    Receiving a newer note does not automatically
    delete older notes stored in the database.
    Your partner may independently copy, photograph
    or share content they receive.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>5. Partner Connections</h2>
  <p>
    HeartLuma allows users to connect privately
    using invitation codes.
  </p>
  <p>
    Invitation codes are designed to expire and
    be usable once. The database stores a hash
    of each invitation code.
  </p>
  <p>
    Authenticated database permissions restrict
    access to information belonging to other couples.
    Your partner does not receive your account password.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>6. Home Screen Widgets</h2>
  <p>
    HeartLuma may display your latest authorized
    received note through an iPhone Home Screen widget.
  </p>
  <p>
    The app and widget share a protected local
    display snapshot using an iOS App Group.
    Authentication credentials are not included
    in the widget snapshot.
  </p>
  <p>
    Local widget information is cleared or invalidated
    when the app detects logout or relevant account
    and partner-connection changes.
  </p>
  <p>
    iOS controls widget refresh timing. A previously
    rendered widget image may remain visible temporarily.
    Other people with access to your device screen
    may see displayed content.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>7. Authentication and Security</h2>
  <p>
    HeartLuma uses Supabase Authentication for
    account registration, email verification,
    sign-in and session management.
  </p>
  <p>
    Saved device sessions are encrypted using
    AES-GCM, with encryption-key protection
    provided through the iOS Keychain.
  </p>
  <p>
    We use authentication requirements and database
    access restrictions to reduce unauthorized access.
    No electronic service can guarantee absolute security.
  </p>
</section>
